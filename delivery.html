Delivery Charges & Payment

📦 Pakistan Orders

Flat-rate courier delivery nationwide. Cash on Delivery (COD) available in most cities, or prepay via bank transfer / JazzCash / Easypaisa.

🌍 International Orders

Shipping cost depends on destination and order weight — we calculate and confirm this with you on WhatsApp before you pay.

💳 How You Pay

International customers can pay via bank transfer, Western Union, or another method we agree on together — confirmed securely over WhatsApp.

Customs & Import Duties

Depending on your country, local customs authorities may apply import duties or taxes on your order. These charges are set by your government and are the responsibility of the customer — they are not included in our product or shipping prices.
